          Attacks

          • Light (jab): fastest, longest reach, 1 damage. Almost no windup.
          • Medium: mid reach, 2 damage. Breaks armor.
          • Heavy (power shot): shortest reach, 3 damage. Armored windup (gold): lights bounce off it. Breaks guard.
          • Triangle: light beats medium (faster) · heavy beats light (armor) · medium beats heavy (faster, breaks armor). Same attack at once = clash.
          • Whiffs recover slower than hits, so a missed attack can be punished.
          • Hit someone during their windup: counter hit (+1 damage).
          • Momentum lunge: moving toward where you aim adds reach; moving away loses reach.
          • Dashed arcs in front of you show each attack's reach (toggle in Tuning).

          Defense and movement

          • Block: front only, slow walk, costs stamina per hit. The blocker recovers first. Heavy breaks it. Out of stamina = guard breaks.
          • Parry: every fresh press of block is a parry attempt for a split second (tap or hold). It stops any attack and stuns the attacker. Miss it and you're either unguarded (tapped) or just blocking (held). Mashing gives no parry, and a block held through a recovery doesn't parry.
          • Steps are not invulnerable. Read where they're going and hit them there. A step adds to your current motion (up to a cap). Forward steps go a bit further, backsteps are shorter.
          • Plant stops you dead; costs more the faster you're moving. During a recovery it only stops your slide.
          • Presses are remembered briefly (input buffer), so an attack or step pressed just before you're free still comes out.
          • Walking away from your opponent is slower than walking toward them. The speed wake behind a fighter shows momentum: green = moving in, red = backing off.
          • ▲ / ▼ next to your stamina bar: ▲ = moving in, stamina refills faster. ▼ = backing off, refills slower. Landing a hit refunds some.
          • After a hit the hitter acts slightly first, and knockback pushes you apart. Only a wall splat gives a guaranteed follow-up (once per combo).
          • Tag team: your partner waits at your corner. Walk into your corner zone (it lights up) and press tag; they come in, you rest. Part of every hit is a bruise (lighter health pips) that heals while you rest. If you're K.O.'d your partner comes in; the round ends when a whole team is down. While waiting you can roam around outside the ring (your tag zone comes with you) and aim. Tag shouts TAG ME IN!. Medium or heavy shoves the other waiting partner if you're aiming at them up close. Light is a jab from outside (one every few seconds): with No DQ rules it can hit the opponent in the ring, with Apron brawl it can hit the other waiting partner. Partners can't walk through each other.
          • Time out: three judges score the round like boxing: damage and clean hits first, then pressure (moving forward), ring control (holding the center) and defense. Landing one hit and running away loses the decision.
